# Radio recheck - 14 November 2026, Australia/Perth

No firmware or saved settings were changed.

## Regional settings

The current WA community page publishes 923.125 MHz, bandwidth 62.5 kHz,
SF8, CR8 and two-byte path hashes. The upstream preset discussion explicitly
identifies Bunbury as using the same modulation settings.

Sources:
- https://perth.meshcore.au/
- https://github.com/meshcore-dev/MeshCore/issues/949
- https://buy.meshmapper.net/ (Bunbury map; not proof of current peer reachability)

## Device observations

A 150-second USB check read the radio registers and sent exactly one standard
flood advert. TX advanced from 1 to 2, confirming hardware/driver transmission
completion. RX stayed zero in that window; the existing receive-error count
stayed one. No return advert or peer acknowledgement was observed.

A subsequent live recheck showed uptime 20,006,348 ms (about 5 h 33 min),
16 received packets, four receive/read errors, 24 completed transmissions,
one Heard entry and six saved contacts. These are cumulative since this boot,
not all generated by the test. Four errors are readData failures; the available
counter alone does not distinguish CRC rejection from other read failures.

Register reads succeeded: frequency word 0x39b20000 (923.125 MHz), sync 0x1424,
boosted gain 0x96, hardware error flags 0x0000. Driver BW62.5/SF8/CR8,
CRC enabled and explicit header agreed. Status 0x52 and driver receive state
both indicated RX. Automatic addition of chat/repeater/room contacts was enabled,
with no additional hop limit and two-byte paths. Maximum loop gap was 7 ms
in the later observation, with no recovered/lost-interrupt indication.

## Conclusion

MCU-to-radio communication, receive processing and transmit completion operate.
The configured profile matches the published Bunbury/WA network profile.
The device is not completely deaf, but 16 packets and one Heard entry over
approximately 5.5 hours do not establish normal sensitivity or discovery rate.
This check cannot certify antenna performance, radiated power, receiver
sensitivity, or successful delivery to a specific Bunbury repeater.

A controlled comparison against the original firmware, preserving a verified
current full-flash backup first, or a known-good nearby peer/RF test source,
would distinguish firmware reception differences from antenna/environment/network
conditions. Such a comparison was not performed in this recheck.

Raw USB logs remain private: radio-recheck-t.log and radio-recheck-latest-t.log.
